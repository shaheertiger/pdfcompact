## How do I sign a PDF without printing it?

Use this page instead of the print, sign, scan routine. Draw your signature on screen, place it on the signature line and download a signed PDF in under a minute. The result looks the same as a scanned signature, without the grey scan background, and the original text stays sharp and searchable.

Three ways to make the signature:

- **Draw:** sign with your finger on a phone, a stylus on a tablet, or the mouse. Strokes are smoothed, and pressure from a stylus makes the line slightly thicker, like ink.
- **Type:** type your name and choose Casual, Classic or Formal script. Fine for many everyday forms, though a drawn signature looks more personal.
- **Upload:** photograph your signature on white paper. **Remove the white background** makes the paper transparent so only the ink lands on the page.

## Is an electronic signature on a PDF legally valid?

For most everyday documents, in most countries, yes, but the rules depend on where you are and what you're signing. This is general information, not legal advice.

| Where | Law | What it means for a signature added here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States | ESIGN Act (2000) and UETA, adopted by nearly every state | A signature can't be refused just because it's electronic. Exceptions include wills, some family-law papers and certain court notices. |
| European Union | eIDAS Regulation | A simple electronic signature can't be denied legal effect just because it's electronic, but only a qualified signature equals a handwritten one automatically. |
| United Kingdom | Electronic Communications Act 2000 and case law | Electronic signatures are generally accepted for contracts; deeds may need a witness. |
| India | Information Technology Act, 2000 | The Act recognizes electronic signatures made by approved methods such as Aadhaar eSign or a Digital Signature Certificate. A pasted signature image isn't one of those, though many organizations accept it for routine forms. Wills, powers of attorney and property sale deeds are excluded. |

In practice: when a landlord, school or employer emails you a PDF and asks you to "sign and send it back", a visual signature is usually exactly what they expect. When a bank, court or government portal asks for a digital signature, use the certificate-based method they name.

## What is the difference between a visual and a digital signature?

A visual signature, which this page makes, is a picture of your signature placed on the page. A digital signature adds a cryptographic certificate to the file that proves who signed and shows if anything changed afterwards. Digital signatures need an identity provider (Adobe, DocuSign, a government eID or a DSC token in India). If you need the document to look final after signing, run it through [Flatten PDF](/flatten-pdf), and add a password with [Protect PDF](/protect-pdf) before emailing it.

## When people sign PDFs

### A lease or rental application

Letting agents often email the tenancy agreement as a PDF. Sign on every page that asks for it, add initials on each page with Every page, and add the date next to the final signature.

### School permission slips and forms

Trip consent forms and school admission forms arrive as PDFs that nobody wants to print. Sign on your phone in a minute and reply to the teacher's email.

### An offer letter or employment contract

Many employers ask you to sign and return the offer letter. Sign it here, check the date format matches the letter (US or UK style), and send it back as a PDF.

### Invoices, delivery notes and timesheets

Freelancers and contractors sign timesheets and delivery confirmations every week. Keep a photo of your signature on your phone and upload it each time with the background removed.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uploaded signature has a grey box around it | Tick Remove the white background, and photograph the signature in good light. | Shadows make the paper look grey, so it isn't removed. |
| The drawn signature looks shaky | Draw larger and slower; it's scaled down when placed. | Small, quick strokes on a phone screen capture fewer points. |
| The signature is too big on the page | Drag the red corner dot inwards. | It starts at a size that's easy to see and grab. |
| I can't drag on my phone, the page scrolls | Press on the signature itself, not the page around it. | Dragging empty page space scrolls, so the page stays usable. |
| "This PDF needs a password to open" | Open it with its password, save an unprotected copy, then sign that. | Encrypted pages can't be changed without the password. |
| The recipient asked for a "digital signature" | Use the certificate-based service they name. | A visual signature doesn't carry a certificate. |
