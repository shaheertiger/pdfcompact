## How do I put DRAFT across every page?

Tap **DRAFT** and press Add watermark: one large diagonal mark at 20 percent opacity goes on every page. For a mark that's harder to crop out, choose **Tiled** and the word repeats across the page in rows that never overlap.

## Can a watermark stop people copying my PDF?

Not on its own. A watermark puts off casual copying, but someone with a PDF editor can delete it. Two steps make that much harder:

1. Run the watermarked file through [Flatten PDF](/flatten-pdf) and choose **Flatten everything**. Each page becomes one image, so the watermark can't be deleted on its own (text stops being selectable too).
2. Add a password and turn off copying and editing with [Protect PDF](/protect-pdf).

## When people watermark PDFs

### Sending a contract for review

Mark every page DRAFT before you email a contract to the other side or your lawyer, so nobody mistakes it for the final version.

### HR documents and salary information

Offer letters and salary reviews are often marked CONFIDENTIAL. Tile the mark so it covers the whole page, then add a password with Protect PDF before emailing.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The watermark is invisible after saving | Switch Layer to On top. | Behind-content marks are hidden by photos, scans and white background boxes. |
| The logo has a white box | Use a transparent PNG. | JPG has no transparency. |
| "This PDF needs a password to open" | Open it with the password, save an unprotected copy, then try again. | Encrypted pages can't be read without the password. |
