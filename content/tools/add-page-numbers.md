## How do I number pages starting from page 2?

Choose **Skip first page**. The cover gets no number and page 2 shows the number in **Start at**, which is 1 by default. If the cover should count (so page 2 shows "2"), set Start at to 2.

## How do I add "Page 1 of 10" to a PDF?

Pick **Page 1 of N** in the Format menu. The total is the last number printed: skip the cover of a 10-page file and pages read "Page 1 of 9" to "Page 9 of 9", or "Page 2 of 10" to "Page 10 of 10" if you start at 2.

## When people number PDF pages

### Court filings and legal bundles

Many courts want consecutively numbered pages in an e-filed bundle. Merge the documents with [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf) first, then number the combined file so the numbering runs straight through. Check your court's rules for position and size.

### Thesis and dissertation submissions

Universities often want no number on the title page. Number from the first chapter with a custom range.
