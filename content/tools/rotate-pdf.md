## How do I rotate a PDF permanently, not just the view?

Most PDF viewers have a rotate button that only changes what you see on screen. Close the file and it's sideways again, and the person you email it to sees it sideways too. This page changes each page's rotation setting inside the PDF and saves a new copy, so the fix travels with the file. The page content itself isn't redrawn, which is why there's no quality loss and the file size stays almost exactly the same: our 12 MB test file grew by 11 bytes.

## How do I fix a scanned PDF that's upside down?

Scanners with a document feeder often produce double-sided scans where every back side is upside down. Choose Even (2, 4, 6…), press 180° and save. If the whole scan is upside down, keep All pages and press 180°. For pages that came out sideways because the paper went in the wrong way, use Left or Right. If your scan put two pages side by side on one sheet, split them first with [Split PDF Pages in Half](/split-pdf-in-half).

## Can I rotate one page and leave the rest?

Yes. Every page has its own left and right arrows. Turn the landscape table on page 14 of a report without touching the portrait pages around it. To rotate a few scattered pages in one go, choose Page list and type them, for example "3, 8-9, 14".

## When people rotate PDFs

### Double-sided scans from an office copier

Scanning a stack of two-sided forms through a copier's document feeder often flips every back side. Rotate the even pages by 180° and the file reads straight through.

### A landscape table inside a thesis or report

University submission portals and examiners expect each page to open readable. Turn the wide tables and charts so they display the right way, then check the order in [Organize PDF](/organize-pdf).

### Phone scans going to an insurer or HR

A claim form or ID photographed sideways is easy to reject. Fix the orientation before you upload it to the insurer's claim portal or send it to HR, so nobody has to turn their head.

### Printing a PDF the right way round

Some printers follow the page orientation saved in the file. Rotating the pages first avoids a stack of sideways printouts and wasted paper.

### Plans and drawings sent as PDF

Architects' plans and site drawings are often saved sideways on an A3 or A1 sheet. Rotate them so they open upright on a tablet on site.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "This PDF is password-protected" | Unlock it with the password first, then rotate the unlocked copy. | A locked file can't be read or changed without its password. |
| "This PDF is encrypted (it has editing restrictions)" | Remove the restriction first, then rotate. | Changing pages in an edit-restricted file would break it. |
| The page turned the wrong way | Press the opposite arrow, or Undo all turns, then save again. | Left turns anticlockwise, right turns clockwise; the preview shows the result before you save. |
| "Outside this file's pages" | Check the page list against the page count shown at the top. | The list includes a page number the file doesn't have. |
| Some previews show only a page number | Use the buttons anyway; they still work. | Previews are drawn for the first 150 pages to keep phones responsive. |
| The page is still slightly crooked | Rescan it straight, or use [Crop PDF](/crop-pdf) to trim the edges. | This tool turns pages in 90° steps; it can't straighten a small tilt. |
