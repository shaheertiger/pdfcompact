## How do I convert a PDF to JPG without losing quality?

Pick the resolution first. A PDF page has no fixed pixel size: text and drawings are stored as shapes and only become pixels when the page is drawn. At 72 DPI an A4 page is 596 × 843 pixels, fine for a thumbnail but blurry when zoomed. At 150 DPI it's 1242 × 1757, sharp on phones and laptops. At 300 DPI it's 2483 × 3513, enough for print. Then leave JPG quality at 85: on our photo page, 85 gave 232 KB and 100 gave 1.14 MB, almost five times bigger for a difference you'd struggle to see.

## How big are the JPG files?

Measured on our test files, A4 pages, quality 85:

| Resolution | Pixels (A4) | Text page | Photo page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 72 DPI | 596 × 843 | 107 KB | 71 KB |
| 150 DPI | 1242 × 1757 | 320 KB | 232 KB |
| 300 DPI | 2483 × 3513 | 821 KB | 678 KB |

Quality changes size a lot on photos: our photo page at 150 DPI was 136 KB at quality 60, 232 KB at 85 and 1.14 MB at 100.

## Can I convert a PDF to JPG on iPhone or Android?

Yes. Open this page in Safari or Chrome, tap the box and choose the PDF from Files, Downloads or Google Drive. Several pages arrive as a ZIP; on iPhone, tap it in Files to unpack it, and on Android most file managers open ZIPs. For a single page, choose it in the page list so you get a JPG straight away that you can save to Photos.

## When people convert PDF to JPG

### Sharing a page on WhatsApp or Instagram

Chats and social apps show images inline, while a PDF is a download most people skip. Convert the event flyer or menu page to JPG at 150 DPI and post it as a picture.

### Job and exam portals that only accept JPG

Some application portals want certificates or ID pages as JPG files with a size cap in KB. Convert the page at 150 DPI, and if it's still too big, shrink it in [Compress Image](/compress-image).

### Slides in PowerPoint or Google Slides

Drop a chart or a page from a report onto a slide as a picture. 150 DPI looks sharp on a projector; use 300 DPI if the slide will be printed.

### Product sheets for an online shop listing

Marketplaces such as Etsy or eBay take images, not PDFs. Convert the spec sheet or brochure page to JPG and add it to the listing photos.

### Previews and thumbnails on a website

Show visitors what a downloadable PDF looks like. Convert page 1 at 72 DPI for a light preview image that loads fast.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "This PDF is password-protected" | Open it with its password and remove the protection first. | The pages can't be drawn without the password. |
| Text looks soft when zoomed | Convert again at 300 DPI, or use PDF to PNG. | 72 and 150 DPI have fewer pixels, and JPG blurs sharp edges slightly. |
| Files are too large | Pick 150 DPI and quality 70–85. | 300 DPI has four times the pixels of 150 DPI. |
| "Too big to draw" warning | Accept the lower DPI, or split the poster-size page first. | Browsers can't draw canvases over 16.7 million pixels, so very large pages are scaled down. |
| The tab reloads on a phone | Convert fewer pages at a time, at 150 DPI. | Mobile browsers give each tab limited memory. |
| I can't open the ZIP | Tap it in Files (iPhone) or a file manager (Android), or choose one page to get a plain JPG. | Several images come packed in one ZIP. |
