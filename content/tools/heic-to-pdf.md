## How do I convert HEIC to PDF on Windows?

Windows can't open HEIC photos without extra codecs, and many PDF converters reject them. This page decodes the HEIC in your browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox load a 1.3 MB decoder once), converts each photo to a high-quality JPEG and places it on a PDF page. The result is a normal PDF that opens anywhere.

## Which quality setting should I use?

| Setting | Each photo becomes | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original | Full resolution, JPEG 92% | Printing, archiving |
| Balanced | Up to 2480 px, JPEG 82% | Upload portals, sharp A4 prints |
| Smaller file | Up to 1600 px, JPEG 70% | Email, forms with a small size cap |

## When people turn iPhone photos into a PDF

### Documents photographed on an iPhone

A lease, a payslip or a signed form photographed for a landlord or employer. Put the pages in order, choose A4 and Balanced, and send one PDF instead of a string of HEIC files they can't open.

### Receipts and claims

Expense systems and insurance claims want one attachment. Convert a batch of receipt photos into one PDF; if it's still too large, run it through [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf).

### Combining with other PDFs

Need the photos added to an existing document? Make the PDF here, then join them with [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First photo is slow | Wait a few seconds; the rest are faster. | The HEIC decoder loads on first use. |
| "Couldn't read" a photo | Share it again from the Photos app, or set Camera to Most Compatible. | The file is damaged or not really HEIC. |
| PDF too big for a portal | Choose Balanced or Smaller file. | Original keeps full-resolution photos. |
| A page is sideways | Tap its rotate button and convert again. | Rare files carry no usable rotation. |
| Tab crashes on a phone | Convert fewer photos at a time. | Decoding large photos needs a lot of memory. |
