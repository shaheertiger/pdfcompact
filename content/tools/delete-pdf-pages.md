## How do I remove pages from a PDF without Acrobat?

You don't need Acrobat. This page copies the pages you keep into a new PDF on your device. It works the same in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on a laptop or a phone.

## How do I delete blank pages from a scanned PDF?

Scroll the thumbnail grid and tap each blank page. When a double-sided scan has a blank back after every sheet, press Even pages to mark pages 2, 4, 6 and so on in one go, then unmark any back that has writing on it. The box keeps the list in sync, for example `2, 4, 6, 8-10`.

## Does deleting pages make the PDF smaller?

Usually, because images and fonts used only by the removed pages are dropped. Measured results:

| File | Pages removed | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10-page text report | 5 of 10 | 48 KB | 34 KB |
| 24-page photo PDF | 22 of 24 | 12 MB | 1.3 MB |

Text-only files shrink less, because every page shares the same fonts. Still too big? Try [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf).

## When people delete PDF pages

### Sending a bank statement to a landlord

A rental application asks for proof of income, not your whole statement. Delete the pages with other accounts and send only what was asked for.

### Removing blank backs from a double-sided scan

Duplex scanners keep the blank backs of one-sided sheets. Remove them before emailing the scan.

### Dropping the cover and terms pages from an invoice

Forward only the pages a finance team needs, without the marketing cover and the standard terms.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Delete button is greyed out | Unmark at least one page. | A PDF can't have zero pages. |
| "Outside this file's pages" | Check the page count in the file bar and fix the number. | The box asks for a page the file doesn't have. |
| "This PDF is password-protected" | Remove the open password first. | It can't be read without that password. |
| The file isn't much smaller | Run it through Compress PDF. | The remaining pages share most of the fonts and images. |
