## How do I compare two versions of a contract?

Drop the version you agreed to as the original and the version you've been sent as the changed file. The summary tells you at once how many changes there are and which ones touch figures: on our test contract it found all four edits, a fee raised from $2,400 to $2,750, a start date moved from 1 February to 1 March 2026, a notice period changed from 30 to 60 days and a new clause 5 ("The client shall not be liable…"), which is flagged as a negation.

This matters because the dangerous edits are small. A changed digit in a price, a date pushed back a month or a single "not" added to a liability clause is easy to miss when you reread 30 pages, and impossible to miss when it's highlighted in red and green.

## How do I find differences between two PDFs without Adobe Acrobat?

Acrobat Pro has a Compare Files feature, but it's part of a paid subscription. This page does the core job in your browser: word-level comparison, a side-by-side view and a redline you can send. Because it runs locally, you can compare files you aren't allowed to upload to a cloud service, such as client contracts, medical policies or HR documents.

## Can I compare scanned PDFs or photos of documents?

Only after OCR. A scanned page is an image, so there is no text to compare, and the tool tells you so instead of reporting "no changes". Run both scans through [OCR PDF](/ocr-pdf), then compare the searchable copies. OCR isn't perfect, so treat a lone changed letter in a scan as a possible misread and check it against the page image.

## When people compare PDFs

### Contract redlines before signing

The other side says they "only fixed typos". Compare their version with the one you sent and check the flagged items first: prices, dates, notice periods and any new "not". Send the redline PDF back with your questions.

### Lease renewals

Landlords often reissue the whole lease at renewal. Compare last year's lease with the new one to see rent changes, a new deposit amount, a different notice period or a clause that now says pets are not allowed.

### Insurance policy changes

Renewal documents rarely list what changed. Compare the old and new policy wording to catch a raised excess, a new exclusion ("this policy does not cover…") or a changed percentage of cover.

### Supplier price list updates

Drop last quarter's price list and the new one; every changed price is flagged as a money amount, so you can see which items went up without checking row by row.

### Thesis and report drafts

Compare the draft your supervisor commented on with your revision to confirm every requested change made it in, or to show a committee exactly what changed between submissions.

| Highlight boxes are slightly off | Use the change list text, which is exact. | Box positions are estimated from the PDF's text positions. |
| Redline PDF won't build | Use the .txt report, or remove the file's restrictions first. | The changed file is encrypted with an owner password. |
| Additions show up as deletions | Press Swap and compare again. | The newer file was put in the Original slot. |
