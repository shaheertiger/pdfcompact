## How do I open HEIC files on Windows?

Windows can only show HEIC photos with extra codecs from the Microsoft Store, and many programs and websites refuse them altogether. Converting to JPG is the simple fix: JPG opens everywhere, from Word and email to government upload forms. This page converts in the browser, so there's nothing to install and the photos aren't sent anywhere.

## HEIC vs JPG: what changes when you convert?

| | HEIC (from iPhone) | JPG after converting at 90% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resolution | 4032×3024 (12 MP) | Same, 4032×3024 |
| File size | Small for its quality | Often larger than the HEIC |
| Opens on Windows, websites, forms | Often not | Everywhere |
| GPS location and camera data | Included | Removed |
| Live Photo motion | Separate video | Not included |

## When people convert HEIC to JPG

### Upload forms that reject HEIC

Job portals, visa applications and school forms usually accept JPG and PNG only. Convert first, then use [Compress Image](/compress-image) if the form also has a size limit, such as a photo under 50 KB.

### Sharing with Windows and Android users

Photos emailed from an iPhone can arrive as HEIC, which older Windows PCs can't preview. Send a ZIP of JPGs instead.

### Making a PDF from iPhone photos

If you want the photos in a PDF rather than as separate JPGs, skip a step and use [HEIC to PDF](/heic-to-pdf).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Couldn't convert this HEIC file" | Open it on the phone and share it again, or export it as Most Compatible. | The file is damaged, or it's not really HEIC. |

| The converter stops responding | Reload the page and convert fewer photos at a time. | Very large batches can run out of memory on phones. |
| JPG is bigger than the HEIC | Lower the quality to 80%, or compress afterwards. | JPG is a less efficient format than HEIC. |
| iPhone already gave me a JPG | Nothing to do; iOS sometimes converts when you pick from Photo Library. | Safari's photo picker can convert automatically. |
