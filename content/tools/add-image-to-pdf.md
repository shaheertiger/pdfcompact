## How do I add a logo to every page of a PDF?

Add the logo, drag it to a corner and size it, then select it and tap **Every page** under Put it on. It is placed at the same relative position on every page, so it stays in the corner on A4, Letter or landscape pages alike. For a faint background mark across the whole page instead, [Watermark PDF](/watermark-pdf) is quicker.

## Which image format should I use?

| Image | Best format | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company logo | PNG with transparent background | No white box around it |
| Passport or ID photo | JPG | Smallest file for photos |
| Rubber stamp or seal | PNG | Sharp edges, transparency |
| Handwritten signature | PNG, or use Sign PDF | Sign PDF can remove the paper background |
| iPhone photo (HEIC) | Convert to JPG first | Browsers can't read HEIC here |

## When people add images to PDFs

### A logo on quotes and invoices

Freelancers and small businesses often make quotes in a template without branding. Add the logo to the top corner of the first page, or every page, before sending it to a client.

### A passport photo on an application form

Many application forms, such as visa, exam and job forms that arrive as PDFs, have a box for a photo. Add the JPG, drag it into the box and shrink it to fit. Check the form's own photo rules (size, background) first; [Resize Image](/resize-image) can crop the photo to the right shape.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A white box shows around the logo | Use a PNG with a transparent background. | JPG has no transparency. |
| The photo appears sideways | Re-add it; phone photos are turned upright automatically. If it persists, rotate it in your gallery and save a copy. | Some photos store rotation in a tag the tool reads. |
| "HEIC photos aren't supported" | Convert it with HEIC to JPG first. | Browsers can't decode HEIC here. |
| "This PDF needs a password to open" | Unlock it in your PDF reader first. | Encrypted pages can't be changed without the password. |
