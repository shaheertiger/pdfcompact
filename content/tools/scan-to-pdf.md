## How do I scan a document without a scanner?

Your phone's camera is enough. A modern phone camera captures 12 megapixels or more, far more detail than a page needs. What makes a photo look like a scan is the clean-up: the Document filter estimates the paper brightness across the page, removes shadows and uneven light, then makes the paper white and the text dark. Each page is saved as a JPEG at up to 2480 pixels on the long side, about 210 DPI on A4, which is sharp for printing and reading but keeps the file small.

## Which filter makes the best scan?

| Filter | Best for | Size per A4 page in our tests | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Document | Letters, forms, contracts, receipts | 180–245 KB | Removes shadows, white paper, dark text, keeps grey shades |
| Black & white | Plain typed text, smallest files | 55–75 KB | Pure black and white; faint pencil may disappear |
| Original | Photos, coloured stamps, ID cards | 160–245 KB | Keeps the photo as taken, only cropped and rotated |

You can set one filter for all pages and override it on single pages, for example Black & white for text pages and Original for a page with a coloured logo.

## How do I scan several pages into one PDF?

Keep capturing: every photo becomes the next page. You can add more pages from the camera or from your photo library at any time, move pages with the arrow buttons and delete retakes. When you press Create PDF, all pages go into one file in the order shown. To add a scanned page to an existing PDF, make the scan here, then combine both in [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf).

## When people scan to PDF

### Rental applications

Landlords and letting agents ask for a signed application, ID, payslips and bank statements, often as one PDF by email. Scan everything in one go, use Document for the paperwork and Original for your ID, and send one tidy file instead of ten photos.

### Expense receipts

Finance teams want receipts as PDFs, not phone photos. Choose Fit to image for long till receipts so they aren't shrunk onto an A4 page, scan a week's receipts into one file, and tick OCR so you can search for a vendor later.

### School forms and permission slips

Print, sign, scan and email it back to the teacher before the deadline. Black & white keeps a one-page form around 60 KB, small enough for any school email system.

### IRCC and visa supporting documents

Canadian immigration (IRCC) and visa portals ask for supporting documents as files with a size cap per upload. Scan each document separately, use Document or Black & white to stay small, and check the limit shown on the upload page. If a file is still too big, run it through [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf).

### Signed forms returned by email

When someone sends a form to sign by hand, scan the signed pages and reply with a PDF. A 10-page scan is usually under 2.5 MB, far below Gmail's 25 MB and Outlook's 20 MB attachment limits. To sign without printing, use [Sign PDF](/sign-pdf) instead.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Camera access was blocked" | Allow the camera for this site in browser settings, or use Take a photo. | The browser asks permission before a page can use the camera. |
| The live camera doesn't start | Tap Take a photo; it opens your camera app and works on every phone. | Some browsers and in-app browsers (inside Instagram or Facebook) don't allow live camera on web pages. |
| The page looks tilted | Retake it with the phone held flat above the page. | There's no automatic perspective correction; crop is rectangular. |
| Text looks faint or broken | Switch that page to Document or Original instead of Black & white. | Black & white drops light grey strokes such as pencil or faded print. |
| Dark blotches around the page | Crop to the edge of the paper. | The filter treats the table under the page as paper and brightens it. |
| A photo won't open | Pick JPG or PNG, or convert HEIC files with [HEIC to PDF](/heic-to-pdf). | Some browsers can't decode HEIC photos copied from an iPhone. |
| The phone tab reloads | Scan in batches of about 20 pages and merge them. | Mobile browsers give each tab limited memory. |
