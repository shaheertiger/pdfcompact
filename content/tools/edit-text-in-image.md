## How do I edit text in an image online without the original file?

You have a banner, flyer or screenshot with a typo, and the designer's file is long gone. Retyping the whole graphic is overkill; you need the word fixed. This tool does it in three steps: it reads the text with OCR (Tesseract, running in your browser), lets you tap the word, and repaints that box. The background is sampled from a thin ring just outside the word, the text colour from the strongest pixels inside it, and the font size is calculated so your letters are as tall as the old ones.

In our tests on clean banners and screenshots, the OCR found every word, including white text on a dark blue background, and the matched size was within 1–4 pixels of the original (40 px text came out at 40–41 px, 64–72 px headings at 68–76 px).

## Will the edit be noticeable?

That depends almost entirely on the background behind the text.

| Background | Result | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One flat colour (banners, slides, app screenshots) | Usually invisible | Background colour |
| Gentle gradient or soft shadow | Usually invisible | Smart fill |
| Photo, texture, pattern | Visible smooth patch | Smart fill, or crop or blur the area elsewhere |
| Text touching other text or lines | Edges of neighbours may be covered | Draw a tighter box |

The font is the other giveaway. The tool offers seven common families and approximates; it doesn't recognise fonts. For short fixes (a date, a number, a misspelled word) that's rarely visible. For a long headline in a distinctive display font, it will be.

## How do I replace a word without it overlapping the next one?

Pick Lines instead of Words and retype the whole line, so the spacing is redrawn from scratch. Or tick Fit to box width, which squeezes or stretches the new text to exactly the old width (small changes look fine; big ones look condensed). The editor warns you when the new text is noticeably wider than the box.

## When people edit text in a picture

### A typo in a social media banner or ad

A misspelled word on an Instagram graphic or a Facebook event banner, and the Canva or Photoshop file belongs to someone else. Tap the word, fix it, save as PNG and re-upload. If you need to fit the platform's size afterwards, use [Resize Image](/resize-image).

### An old date or price on a flyer or menu

Event flyers and menus are reused with "2025" or last year's price. Change the number and keep everything else as it was.

### Screenshots for documentation or support tickets

Hide an email address, an order number or a customer name in a screenshot before sharing it: tap the text, clear the field and apply. The text is covered, not blurred, so it can't be recovered from the image.

### Slides exported as images

Presentation slides saved as JPG or PNG often have plain backgrounds, which is the ideal case. Fix a name, title or figure before the slide goes into a report.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The word has no box | Press Draw a box and drag across it. | OCR misses stylised, curved, tiny or handwritten text. |
| A visible patch shows | Try Smart fill, or pick the fill colour yourself. | Busy or textured backgrounds can't be rebuilt from the edges. |
| New text runs into the next word | Use Lines, tick Fit to box width, or lower the size. | The replacement is longer than the original. |
| Text colour is wrong | Pick it with the Text colour control. | Thin or anti-aliased letters can sample lighter than they look. |
| Font looks different | Try another family and toggle Bold or Italic. | Fonts are approximated, not identified. |
| "The text reader couldn't start" | Reload the page with a working connection, or draw boxes by hand. | The 7 MB reader files didn't download. |
| Very large photo is slow | Resize it first with Resize Image. | OCR time grows with the number of pixels. |
