## How do I split scanned book pages into single pages?

Scan the book open on the glass, two pages per scan, and save it as one PDF. Drop it here, keep Left | Right, and tick Keep the first page whole if the first scan is the front cover on its own. On our test file of landscape A4 spreads, every spread became two portrait A5-sized pages (148 × 210 mm), and the text on each new page was the correct half: "Page 1" on the first, "Page 2" on the second, and so on.

## Left | Right or Top / Bottom?

| Your page looks like | Choose | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An open book: two pages side by side | Left \| Right | Two narrower pages |
| Two pages one above the other | Top / Bottom | Two shorter pages |
| A manga or Arabic book spread | Left \| Right + right-to-left | Right page first |
| A single cover, then spreads | Either + keep first page whole | Cover stays whole |

## When people split pages in half

### Scanned textbooks and library books

Students scanning chapters on a library copier end up with two pages per sheet. Splitting makes the PDF readable on a phone or e-reader and lets you print only the pages you need. Then use [Crop PDF](/crop-pdf) to trim the dark book edge.

### Comics and manga

Tick right-to-left order and keep the cover whole so the pages read in the original order in any PDF reader.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix | Why it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Words near the spine are cut | Set the overlap to 1–3%. | The book wasn't centred exactly on the scanner. |
| Pages come out in the wrong order | Toggle right-to-left order, or check the cover option. | The book reads right to left, or the cover was cut in half. |
| Very narrow pages | Choose Top / Bottom instead. | The pages were portrait, so they weren't side-by-side spreads. |
| One side has a wide margin | Crop odd and even pages separately with Crop PDF. | The cut is always in the exact middle of the page. |
| "Needs a password to open" | Remove the open password first. | The file can't be read without it. |
